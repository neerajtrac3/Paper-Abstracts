# Paper Abstracts and Publications

A structured collection of abstracts, drafts, and publication notes for articles and papers on banking, AI, and modernization.

## Repository structure

- `abstracts/` – Abstracts for in-progress or proposed papers
- `published/` – Summaries and links to published work
- `drafts/` – Early ideas and topic backlogs

## Purpose

To maintain a clear, versioned record of your research and publication pipeline, aligned with your professional and academic work.
